# Sevvn Site Wireframe

Last updated: 2026-07-26

## Home

1. Header
   Goal: orient brand, navigation, login and main commercial CTA

2. Hero
   Goal: reposition Sevvn immediately as platform, not software house

3. Muito mais do que um aplicativo
   Goal: explain the guest app as one interface inside a larger system

4. O que muda para o seu hotel
   Goal: translate features into operational and commercial outcomes

5. Jornada do hóspede
   Goal: show the guest lifecycle with explicit public status per stage

6. Ecossistema Sevvn
   Goal: visually represent guest app, hotel portal, Sevvn admin, integrations, data and network

7. Produtos e interfaces
   Goal: introduce Sevvn Guest, Sevvn Hotel, Sevvn Platform and Sevvn Network

8. Plataforma modular
   Goal: explain plan -> template -> modules -> brand -> integrations -> published experience

9. Recursos para toda a jornada
   Goal: summarize public feature catalog by business purpose and status

10. Templates
    Goal: reinforce that 5 templates are visual identities of the same platform
    Note: no screenshots in the Home implementation; mention their existence and link to deeper page

11. Rede Sevvn
    Goal: present the partner-network vision honestly as under development

12. Casos de uso
    Goal: show conceptual scenarios for boutique inn, urban hotel, resort and chain

13. Roadmap resumido
    Goal: show Available / In development / Coming soon with clear honesty

14. Planos
    Goal: show Essential, Premium and Enterprise as maturity tiers

15. Clientes e parceiros fundadores
    Goal: invite early adopters and strategic partners without artificial scarcity

16. FAQ
    Goal: remove objections and clarify stage, compatibility and growth path

17. CTA final
    Goal: convert hotels and partners at the end of the narrative

18. Footer
    Goal: close brand, contact, legal and login/admin access
